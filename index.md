# Wickbound: Carry the Light - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Wickbound: Carry the Light ("the game") is a mobile game made by Half Moon Gaming ("we", "us").

## Summary

The game does not collect, share, or sell any personal information. It works entirely on your device and does not connect to the internet.

## What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, and high scores are saved locally on your device so you can continue playing. We cannot see this data. Uninstalling the game removes it.

## Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers. This version of the game has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no in-app purchases.

## Third parties

The game does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking tools. Google Play may collect its own information when you download the game, under Google's own privacy policy.

## Children

The game is intended for ages 13 and up. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version adds ads, purchases, or any data collection, we will update this policy before releasing that version and change the date above.
